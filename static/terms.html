1. What Wilma is

Wilma is an API that classifies message text and returns a fraud verdict. It is decision support. It is not a decision, and it does not replace your own fraud controls or your obligations to your customers.

2. Pilot status

Wilma is in pilot. There is no service level agreement, no guaranteed uptime, and the models may change without notice as they improve. We will tell pilot customers when a model changes, because it may change your results.

3. Your API key

Keep it secret. You are responsible for calls made with your key. Tell us immediately if it is exposed and we will revoke and reissue it. We store only a hash of your key, so we cannot recover it for you.

4. Acceptable use

Use Wilma to screen messages for fraud. Do not use it to build a competing classifier from our outputs, to attack the service, to exceed rate limits deliberately, or to process data you have no lawful right to process.

5. Your data

You keep all rights in the content you send. We do not store message content and we do not train on it. See the privacy policy. Where the content includes personal data of your customers, you are the controller and we are your processor.

6. Accuracy

No fraud classifier is correct every time and ours is not either. We publish our measured accuracy, including our failures, so you can judge it. You accept that Wilma will sometimes flag a legitimate message and sometimes miss a fraudulent one, and you agree to design your own systems on that assumption.
